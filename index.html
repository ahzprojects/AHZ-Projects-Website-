About Us

AHZ Projects began in Steelpark, Vereeniging with two trucks — a 6m³ rubble removal truck and our well-known yellow furniture removals truck, driven by our ever-jovial owner, Craig. From the start, we built a strong reputation serving homes, complexes, and schools across Three Rivers, Vanderbijlpark, Roshnee, Duncanville, and surrounding areas.

Today, we’ve grown into a trusted Vaal-based construction and transport company, offering tipper truck hire, rubble removal, building materials supply, renovations, and landscaping services including instant lawn and turf installation.

As a family-run business, we pride ourselves on fast service, honest work, and lasting client relationships — delivering quality results that keep our customers coming back.
